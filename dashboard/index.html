🐖 Add a New Swine Pen (ESP32 Node)

To attach a new ESP32 hardware controller to your account, follow these simple steps using the device's setup portal:

  1. Power on the new ESP32 node near its swine pen.
  2. Connect your phone or laptop Wi-Fi to the ESP32 setup hotspot:
    IOS-Setup-XXXX (default password: swine1234).
  3. Open your browser to http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Go to Device Settings > Wi-Fi Station and connect it to your farm router.
  5. Under Cloud Setup, enter your account username and password, specify the Pen Assignment (e.g. Pen 2 / Farrowing), and tap Connect to Account.
💡 Physical Security: Devices can only be linked by physically connecting to their Wi-Fi hotspot, ensuring unauthorized accounts cannot claim your farm nodes.
